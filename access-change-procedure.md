Access Change / Role Transfer Procedure

Purpose
Ensure access follows a person's current job responsibilities when they change roles or departments.

Workflow
1. Receive a documented role-change request.
2. Verify the effective date and approval.
3. Compare current permissions with the destination role's approved access profile.
4. Identify permissions that are no longer needed.
5. Remove obsolete access and assign approved new access in accordance with the change plan.
6. Test required access and verify that removed permissions no longer work.
7. Record the actions, timestamps, approver, and verification results.

Example
A fictional Help Desk employee transfers to HR. Remove Help Desk-specific access that is no longer required, then grant only the HR permissions approved for that employee's new duties.

Security Notes
Avoid accumulating permissions over time. Consider whether any permissions create a conflict of duties. Follow company change controls for sensitive systems.
